Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

What the app is

Channel stats is a private tool that a single creator runs on their own computer to read the analytics of YouTube channels they own or manage. It is not offered to other people.

What data it accesses

With the creator's consent it uses two read-only Google permissions:

  • YouTube Analytics (read-only) — views, watch time, average view duration, subscribers gained and lost, traffic sources, thumbnail impressions and click-through rate of the creator's channels and videos.
  • YouTube account (read-only) — the id and name of the channel that was signed in, used only to check that the right channel was chosen.

It cannot upload, change or delete anything on YouTube or in the Google account.

How the data is used and stored

The numbers are written into reports on the creator's own computer. The sign-in token is stored only on that computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the creator's computer removes the stored copy.

Google API Services

Channel stats' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
